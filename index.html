Hi, I'm Del

I've spent 15 years leading data and analytics teams: building reporting from scratch, running data governance, and turning messy numbers into decisions a board will trust.

In that time I've watched the same thing happen again and again. A new ERP, a move off legacy systems, a new reporting platform: most transformation projects don't stumble on the technology. They stumble on the basics. Data is the output of your processes, so when a process is missing, broken or owned by nobody, the data it produces isn't fit for purpose. Most businesses have plenty of data. The trouble is that it's poor quality, or it's read in different ways, so nobody agrees on the numbers. If you've lived through one of those projects, you'll know.

AI is the newest transformation, and it rests on exactly the same basics. If they're shaky, AI won't fix them. It will scale them. The audit is the hour I wish every business spent before buying AI.
